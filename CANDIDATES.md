# Robot Vacuum Excuse candidates — scored in-session

Competition: The Robot Vacuum's Best Excuse (u-golden-heron-44-the-robot-vacuum-s-best-excuse-53412214).
Rules: one line, at most 140 characters, the funniest excuse a robot
vacuum gives for missing a spot. Judge (a tired homeowner) weighs, in
order: (1) one-line excuse from a robot vacuum for a missed spot,
(2) laugh-out-loud funny, (3) rooted in the vacuum's own world
(sensors, bumpers, dock, map, battery), (4) clean turn at the end on
first read, (5) sounds like the vacuum itself, straight-faced.
Scored out of 25: surprise 5 + specific 5 + concise 5 + judge fit 10.

1. "My sensors detected the spot, classified it as 'probably haunted', and I have elected not to engage." — 21/25. Funny, sensor-rooted, but "elected not to engage" is stiff.
2. "I skipped that corner because my map says it belongs to the dust bunny, and I respect local wildlife." — 22/25. Good punch, map-rooted, but "dust bunny" is a familiar gag.
3. "That spot is outside my coverage area. I have filed a formal complaint with my own map." — 20/25. Decent, but the complaint lands soft.
4. "My bumper touched the chair leg, my cliff sensor saw the rug edge, and I decided the spot could keep itself." — 19/25. Rooted, but three beats and a weak ending.
5. "I missed it because my battery was at 4% and I had to choose between the crumb and making it home to my dock." — 21/25. Battery/dock-rooted, clear stakes, punch slightly explains itself.
6. "The spot moved. I have the lidar logs to prove it. I am not going back in there." — 20/25. Lidar is a nice touch, ending is generic.
7. "I cleaned around it out of respect — that dust has been there longer than I have, it has seniority." — 22/25. Great punch, vacuum voice, but "out of respect" softens the excuse.
8. "My dustbin was full, my filter was clogged, and honestly that corner looked like it was winning." — 20/25. "Looked like it was winning" is a good punch, setup is a list.
9. "I attempted the spot three times. Each time my wheels spun, my sensors lied, and the spot remained. I have made my peace." — 19/25. Too long a story for one line; the ending is flat.
10. "That spot is not dirt. According to my sensors it is a load-bearing crumb, and I will not be the one to test it." — 24/25. WINNER. Surprising and specific, sensors-rooted, clean turn at the very end, straight-faced vacuum voice, 117 chars.
